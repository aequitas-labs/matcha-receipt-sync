# Chrome Web Store Listing

## Short Description

_(132 characters max)_

Export and sync receipts from Amazon, Costco, Walmart, and Target to CSV/JSON or directly to matcha money.

---

## Long Description

Understand your data. **Develop your ritual.**

Automatically capture order histories from your favorite big box retailers. Free your purchase data as a clean CSV or JSON file, or sync it seamlessly into matcha money.

EXPORT WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

No account required. Simply browse to your order history on any supported retailer, and the extension will automatically detect and extract your receipts. Download them as:

• CSV — open in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers
• JSON — for developers or custom workflows

Your data stays on your device until you choose to export it.

SYNC TO MATCHA MONEY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

matcha money (https://matcha.money) is a personal finance platform designed to help you develop your ritual for financial wellness. It connects to your bank accounts and credit cards, automatically categorizes transactions, and helps you build and manage a realistic budget — including itemized purchase data from this extension.

Syncing receipts to matcha money requires a matcha money subscription. Exporting to CSV or JSON is always free and requires no account.
